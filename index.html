Professional Biography

Education, Technological Adoption & Vision

Hello! I am Tanvi Dubey, an energetic student from Agra, Uttar Pradesh, born on 10 November 2007. I am currently pursuing my Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Logistics from the esteemed Dr. MPS Memorial Group of Institutions in Agra.

My focus centers on the operational engines that power contemporary commerce: supply chain management, warehouse workflows, and multi-modal distribution. In parallel, I actively leverage modern AI tools (including generative text systems, Canva AI, and research copilots) alongside core computer suites (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) to optimize productivity, draft precise documentation, and present impactful ideas.

Beyond academia, I am an enthusiastic athlete and creative performer. On the court, I play badminton and basketball, both of which have taught me sharp reflexes, endurance, and the value of selfless team coordination. In moments of creative expression, dance and listening to music keep me harmonious, focused, and energized.

Guiding Philosophy: "Consistency in preparation, agility in sports, and smart utilization of AI tools create a balanced path to leadership and professional excellence."
